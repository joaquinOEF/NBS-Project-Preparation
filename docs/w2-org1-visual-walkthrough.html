The run

What this run says

Every deliberate behaviour fired: the chips ordered themselves by the bairro data, the scale message appeared for a story about 2024 and would have stayed silent otherwise, the photo requests followed her stated worry, her correction outranked the raster and was quoted back in the recommendation, and all five families shipped with the weak ones grouped instead of cut. Three photos uploaded in a single action, which was impossible this morning.

What still deserves attention is mostly the map: the legend that names three colours for a five-colour map, the flood ramp where green means danger two taps before green means calm, the three input modes offered at once for marking a site, and the tap target itself — the one interaction an automated harness could not hit reliably. Those are what the map redesign addresses. The role chips also still bundle "receber e administrar recursos" where the July taxonomy separates them.
