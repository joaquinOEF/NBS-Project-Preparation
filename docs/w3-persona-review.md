# Three readers on the current Encontro 3

Run against a full transcript of the richest path (Horta Raízes do Sarandi,
jardim de chuva, 500 m², mutirão com apoio técnico) as it stands today, not
against a description of it. The three readers are the ones actually on this
programme's record: a technical reviewer, a field facilitator, a programme lead.

Ordered by what I would fix first.

---

## Robson — technical review

**R1 · The price ignores who builds it, and we ask who builds it.**
The session captures `construction_model` — *mutirão*, *empresa contratada*,
*parceria* — one beat after showing the cost band, and the band never moves.
The catalogue itself knows this matters: `teto-verde`'s own note says the
bidim/mutirão method drops it from R$150–350/m² to about **R$5/m²**, and cites
Teto Verde Favela covering twenty roofs that way. A 30–70× spread, captured and
discarded.

An organisation that answered *mutirão* is printing a contractor's price in its
concept note. That is the single least defensible number W3 currently produces.

**R2 · The study is named and never costed.**
*"precisa de um teste de infiltração do solo"* appears in the verdict and in the
shortlist card, and nowhere in the budget. A concept note with a works cost and
no study line is incomplete — and that line is precisely what the coordination
is pooling across the cohort, so its absence weakens the portfolio case too.

**R3 · Nothing states what the solution needs to keep working.**
Maintenance frequency is asked, cost of maintenance is asked in one custom
question, but the ficha's own operating requirement never reaches the page.

## Ana — field facilitation

**A1 · The shortlist does not help anyone choose.**
All four options read:

> *Responde ao que vocês contaram — pra água que junta e não escoa*

The differentiator sits in the tail, in smaller type: one needs an infiltration
test, one needs a técnico, one needs a hydrological study, one is *custo alto*.
An organisation choosing between four things that look identical picks the first,
and the choice this whole encontro exists to make is made by ordering.

The fix is not more text. It is one contrasting clause per card — what this one
gives you that the next one does not.

**A2 · The heaviest paragraph lands at the lightest moment.**
Immediately after choosing, before the size question, the organisation is handed
four sentences of regulatory prose (`quemPrecisaDizerSim`: SMAMUS, DMAE, private
lot, public square, drainage connection). It is correct and it is unreadable at
that point in the session. It belongs in the dossier, where they read it with the
coordination — with one line here.

**A3 · The scale statement and the "parece pouco" reply now say the same thing.**
Both explain that NBS do not solve 2024. Two paragraphs, one point, back to back.

---

## Ricardo — programme

**P1 · The organisation is told about "a rede inteira" and never sees it.**
The new scale statement's whole argument is *alone a fraction of a percent,
together a number* — and the organisation has no way to see the together. The
synergy report exists and is coordinator-only. If the case for the programme is
being made to the organisations, some version of it has to be visible to them.

**P2 · Nothing shows them what they have until the end.**
The dossier and the hoja de ruta arrive at the close. Through a session of
fifteen beats there is no running answer to *"what do we have so far?"* — which
is what someone asks when they are deciding whether to keep going.

**P3 · The four maturity scores still never run.**
`problem_clarity`, `solution_clarity`, `climate_nbs_impact`,
`financial_thinking`. The close gate exists and nothing calls `score_maturity`,
so every organisation leaves W3 with an unscored record and the coordinator's
roster shows nothing for the encontro that produced the most.

## What was already fixed while writing this

The scale statement was landing **after** the reaction chips — the organisation
was asked *"o que vocês acham desse número?"* and only then told what the number
is a fraction of. Asking for a judgement while withholding the thing it turns
on. Corrected in the same commit as this file.

## Order I would take them

1. **R1** — a wrong number is worse than a missing one, and this one is printed.
2. **A1** — it decides the choice the encontro exists to make.
3. **R2** — completes the budget and strengthens the pooling case.
4. **P3** — cheap, and the coordinator's roster is blank without it.
5. **A2 / A3** — reading load.
6. **P1 / P2** — the largest, and the least urgent before September.
