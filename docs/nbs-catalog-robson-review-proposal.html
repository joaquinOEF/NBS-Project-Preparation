What Robson actually said — five cross-cutting themes

The 38 comments are one coherent argument, not scattered notes:

1 · Complexity is the feasibility ceiling

"Very important for feasibility. The high complexity NbS are (almost) not feasible." Everything licensed/engineered is effectively out of a CBO's 1–2 year reach.

2 · Scale is a hidden axis

Several solutions fail not on cost but on scale: constructed wetlands, linear parks, reforestation and wetland restoration "fit to landscapes, not neighborhoods." Urban Green Infrastructure as a whole is "usually for neighborhoods and larger areas — important for scale."

3 · Some solutions aren't quite NbS

Retention basins, rainwater harvesting, biodigester kitchens, geocells: "not 100% NbS, but still useful." He never says remove — he says label honestly.

4 · Licensing is concrete, not abstract

Street bioswales → check water & gas pipes; permeable pavement on streets → EPTC + SMAMUS approval (private terrain: none); rain terraces → Defesa Civil; all natural lakes and rivers are public under Brazilian law.

5 · Feasibility will be dual-input

(Jul 30) His maturity/feasibility mapping lands end of August, combining platform-captured data with direct mentorship — the catalog's feasibility field feeds that, it doesn't replace it.

From Jul 8, two standing constraints on how we encode any of this: don't over-instrument the system with sensitive socio-economic data (tenure, income) — surface those in workshop conversation instead; and asset/tenure/political factors stay with humans, the platform supplies geospatial context and scoring.

Proposed change, in W2 order of value

  • a. Sample the mechanism at the confirmed site/bairro. When the org confirms the site (Mapa 2 → site card), sample the dominant mechanism class (point) and the bairro's mechanism mix (the endpoint exists). One new sampling call — the rasters and decode path are already in the platform.
  • b. Name the mechanism in the "why" lines. Today: "Enchente é alto no Sarandi." With mechanisms: "Aqui o alagamento vem da chuva que não infiltra — não do arroio subindo." This is Ana's Jul 16 framing operationalized: orgs already know their territory; the platform's job is formalizing why, and mechanism language is the credibility jump. Cheap (template per class), high trust value, no re-scoring risk.
  • c. Pick example solutions by mechanism × feasibility. exampleSolutionsFor currently sorts by delivery/cost only. Port the affinity table from nbs_rules.py to a shared TS map (mechanism class → solution boost): pluvial/drainage_constrained → jardins, biovaletas, pavimentos; riverine/low_lying → restauração de áreas úmidas, parques lineares; shade_deficit → corredores/parques; steep_activatable_slope + vegetation_deficit → grade viva, muro verde. Combined with P1's feasibility field, the two examples shown per família become buildable AND mechanism-right.

Keep out of W2: that's scoping territory, and Robson's data-minimalism caution applies to the social-exposure classes.

Refined design (2026-07-30 /refine — decisions locked)

  • Decision-support, not verdict: the recommendation moment shows all 5 famílias, sorted by fit but none hidden (the top-3 cut is what makes a ranking prescriptive). Each família carries soft signal chips — [neighborhood risk] [mechanism] [what you told us] — instead of a single verdict line; famílias below a signal floor are grouped honestly as "no strong signal for this place", not fake-ranked. "See all 27 solutions" is always one tap away from this screen.
  • Mechanism folds into the ranking as a capped boost (≤0.15, tie-breaker scale — it can reorder near-ties, never bury a high-risk família), with hedged language ("approximate signal"). The cap holds until the hazard↔mechanism correlation test (Q12) shows the specificity is real.
  • Feasibility is weighted in as well — as the third capped signal, and visibly. Each família gets a small boost (≤0.10) from its share of alta-feasibility solutions (Robson's list), surfaced as its own chip — [has solutions you can build] — so the weighting is transparent, never a hidden thumb on the scale. The formula: score = hazard × (1 + mechanism ≤0.15) + context + feasibility ≤0.10 — hazard stays dominant, so a high-risk família can never be buried by low feasibility, only edged in near-ties. At solution level Robson's list also drives honest badges (baixa renders as "🏛️ needs the government on board", never "can't be done"), the org-toggleable "feasible for organizations" filter (default ON), and example picks = mechanism-fit × feasibility.
