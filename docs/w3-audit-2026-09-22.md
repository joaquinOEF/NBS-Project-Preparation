# Encontro 3 audit — 22 September 2026

A read-only audit of the W3 flow a week before the 30 September convening, run
alongside the Caldas Junior fixes (#558–#561, see the handoff note at the end of
this file). The question it asks is the one that handoff ended on: *every
function did what it said, and nothing compared one function's output to
another's.* So this audit compares them — the community side against the
coordinator side, card against board, what E3 writes against what every surface
reads — over two real staging records.

## What W3 is for, now

**Encontro 3 is a deliberation step, not project scoping.** The organisation
says what weighs most (≤ 2 criteria), tests 2–4 of the 27 solutions — each a
deterministic card, then *quem faria?*, *o que mais pega?* and one reaction —
and leaves with a **comparison** ordered by its own criteria and a *"Pra levar à
mesa do portfólio"* hand-off. Who builds, when, who maintains and the recurring
money are a PROJECT's questions and belong to Encontro 4, designed with what the
room decides on 30 September (#553, JVP 21 Sept).

**Who it is for on 30 September:** the room first — Robson and a facilitator at
a projector with each organisation (Vila Flores's plan, backlog #35) — with the
organisation alone on a phone as the fallback path. Every finding below says
which mode it affects when that matters.

### What E3 writes by default (tail off)

`solution_tests_json` (per test: reaction, `areaM2` / `units`, who, hardest,
decisive detail) → `chosen_solutions` derived from the liked ones ·
`_choice_criteria` + `choice_criteria` · `intervention_site.studies_done` ·
`_document_notes_json` · `dig_json` (round 1) · at close `_e3_closed`,
`project_verdict`, `project_capacity_grade`, three of four maturity scores ·
legacy single-value fields filled by the first liked test: `intervention_units`,
`detail_answer`, `expected_impact`.

## Findings

Ranked by what the 30 September room would see. ✅ = fixed in a PR from this
audit, ↗ = needs a person, ◻ = after 30 September.

### Before 30 September

1. ✅ **#562 — one solution, three prices.** The card priced each solution at its
   test's size; the dossier — so the board, the hoja de ruta and the Resumo —
   priced every solution over the place's footprint and ONE project-wide
   `intervention_units`. Caldas: jardins de chuva R$ 334–585 mil on the card,
   R$ 1,16–2,03 mi on the board. c2a6ab61: 25 trees on the card, 2 on the board
   (escola-verde's count, tested first). `sizeOf()` is now the one rule. #558
   made the card's number better and widened this gap; this closes it.
2. ✅ **#563 — the tail could switch itself back on.** `tailEnabled()` trusted any
   `construction_model`, a declared field a model turn can write. The E3 skill
   the model actually loads still described "Querem detalhar o projeto agora?"
   and told it to write upkeep/money fields; the fallback prompt was the June
   3a/3b/3c workshop. Now the tail head stamps `_tail_enabled`, the skill
   describes the flow the platform runs, and the fallback is a brief.
3. ✅ **#564 — the Documentos tab offered E4 documents as the E3 result**, and the
   Resumo printed the advisor's second-person praise ("Vocês chegam ao Encontro
   3…") in its third-person problem section. Resumo and hoja de ruta now sit
   under "Para o Encontro 4" for records that closed at the comparison.
4. ✅ **#565 — a skip stored as an answer; a trace overwrote the place.** "Prefiro
   pular" at *o que mais pega?* was stored as "nada" and printed "Nada de
   grande". A footprint traced during a test replaced the place's footprint.
5. ↗ **Maturity is not comparable across the roster.** `problem_clarity` reads
   why-here and baseline, which only the tail asks: c2a6ab61 scores 3/3, Caldas
   1/3 while its Resumo quotes the visit report, the sketch and the parents'
   minutes. Real orgs open E3 on the 30th, so all will be scored the post-#553
   way; the risk is test rows beside them. **Decide:** hide phase-3 maturity
   until E4, or bring back why-here + baseline as a confirm of the advisor's
   existing `baseline_condition` draft (handoff open item 3).
6. ↗ **Heat first, water shelf.** Caldas named heat as the focus; the shelf
   opened *"Todas respondem … pra água que desce com força"* and all four tests
   were water solutions. #560 now scores them "~ not your focus" in the
   comparison. Whether the shelf should have seated a heat solution (the
   alignment rule keeps them inside their E2 grupos) is a product question, not
   a bug — worth one look with the room's facilitator.
7. **Operational.** Staging pulls `main` (#558–#565) and restarts; the synergy
   report is regenerated after; Ana presses **Fechar** on E3 before the 30th.

### After 30 September — the cut list

The shape to aim for: **the comparison's per-test card is the one source**, and
every other surface reads it.

- ◻ **Retire `buildDossier` as a pricing and verdict path.** #562 made it agree;
  it is still a second engine. Board pile, Resumo and hoja de ruta should read
  the cards. The board also ignores the `project_verdict` stored at close.
- ◻ **Delete the tail** once E4 is designed: `askConstruction` … `closeE3`,
  the why/baseline/extras/who-pays handlers, draft chips, `parkAtComparison` —
  about 700 of `cboE3Checkpoint.ts`'s ~2,900 lines — and with it the legacy
  chips ("Só essa", "Faz sentido / Parece pouco", "Detalhar agora / Deixar pra
  depois") and `ensureTests` (31 calls). Keep what E4 reuses as its own module.
- ◻ **Retire the single-value fields** (`intervention_units`, `detail_answer`,
  `expected_impact`) once no reader needs them; `sizeOf` already reads per test.
- ◻ **Typed map turns.** Nine `raw.startsWith('Map selection (')` checks and a
  regex on `· N m²` are how a trace is recognised.
- ◻ **Four places write a size, four ways** (measure chip, spoken, rough band,
  map) — one `setTestSize()`.
- ◻ **Waits.** Four points where the user waits on a model pass (shelf 20 s,
  size 25 s, card 25 s per test, comparison 20 s). In the room with a
  facilitator they are dead air on a projector; decide which survive the room
  mode.
- ◻ **Synergy pooling** reads `chosen_solutions` (liked only) while interest
  counts all tests (#557) — decide whether a set-aside solution's study still
  pools.
- ◻ **Stale docs:** `knowledge/runs/2026-06-30-w3-refine/plan.md` and
  `docs/w3-persona-review.md` predate the loop; `docs/cougar-runbook.md` has
  nothing on W3, the synergy button, technical notes or projects.
