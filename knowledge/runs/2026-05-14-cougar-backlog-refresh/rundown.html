The two journeys

Julia (coordinator)

1

Opens /orchestrator

If no coordinator slug in localStorage → sample mode (Vila Flores demo cohort, 4 fake CBOs). For her real cohort: clicks Create cohort.

2

Names the cohort

Single-field Dialog, default "Vila Flores cohort", Enter submits. Server creates cohorts row + seeds 6 workshops. Slug stored to localStorage.

3

Copies "My link"

Recovery key. Share dialog with two buttons: Copy link and Copy WhatsApp message (PT-formatted for the BR audience).

4

Invites 10 CBOs

Per CBO: name + bairro + role (priority/alternate) in one form. Server creates cohort_members row. Share dialog opens with the CBO link + a Portuguese WhatsApp greeting:

"Olá! 👋 Este é o link da plataforma do COUGAR/Vila Flores para Horta Cascata…"

5

Tracks the cohort on the map

Right rail = CBO cards with phase, sections done, maturity, last activity. Hover a card → marker highlights. Toggle Recruit zones → 94 bairros colored by priority score; click → toast with recruit context.

6

After Workshop 2 ends

Clicks Unlock for cohort on the Workshop 2 row. Server appends phase 2 to every member's unlockedPhases. CBOs see phase 2 open on next focus.

Sandra (composite CBO leader, on her phone)

1

WhatsApp message lands

Julia's pre-formatted greeting + a link. Sandra taps.

2

Premium welcome screen #134

Org name in display type, soft emerald gradient, single CTA. Next-workshop card surfaces the date pulled from cohort settings. 5-step preview tells her what's coming.

One tap on Começar → chat begins.

3

Chat flow (single-column on mobile)

Agent guides her through Phase 1: who they are, sector, scale. Multi-choice + free-text. Profile saves automatically; she can leave and come back.

Friendly progress strip at the top: "Section 1 of 5 · Who you are" with 5 segments. Locked segments show 🔒.

4

Tries to skip to Phase 2

Taps the locked segment → Popover (works on touch): "Opens after Workshop 2 · quarta, 11 de junho."

5

Coordinator unlocks Phase 2

Sandra returns to the page later (window focus) → page re-fetches; Phase 2 segment is now tappable; the popover dies. Chat continues with new questions.

6

Profile state syncs back

Every phase_change or maturity_update event pushes a snapshot to PATCH /api/cbo-member/:slug/snapshot. Julia's orchestrator view reflects it on her next refresh.
