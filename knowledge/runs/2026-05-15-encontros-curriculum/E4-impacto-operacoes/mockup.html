E4 — Impacto · operações · sustentabilidade · mockup walkthrough · 2026-05-15

The encontro where an idea becomes a serious project. Three beats over ~50 min: computed impact (ranges, with assumptions visible) → 3-phase operations (Year 1 / Year 2-3 / Year 3+) → sustainability model (5-pattern picker).

Core design call: "Show what's computable, ask what's not." The ImpactCalculator is a deterministic computation over a YAML — not an LLM call — so outputs are reproducible. Operations data only the CBO knows is asked. That separation is why this fits in 50 min.

ImpactCalculator · NEW (the biggest new component)

Deterministic computation, not an LLM call. Reads from _impact-coefficients/by-intervention.yaml (new data file — every coefficient cited).

4 cards filtered by hazard: water (flood), cooling (heat), carbon (all veg), pessoas (all). Each card renders a range with units; "Ver detalhes →" expands to show site characteristics used + literature source.

After the cards, agent invites a 1-sentence monitoring plan (feeds Climate NBS Impact maturity scoring).

OperationsDesigner · NEW

3 collapsible sections — Year 1, Year 2-3, Year 3+. Cost auto-computed per phase using _operations-templates/<intervention>.yaml (intervention's per-phase R$/m²/yr range × area).

Per-phase questions are deliberately different — Year 1 asks about team composition, Year 2-3 asks "who stays" (the death-of-projects moment per community-garden lit), Year 3+ asks about governance model.

Bottom: live 5-year OPEX total. Framed as a sanity check, not a judgment.

component: OperationsDesigner inputs: intervention_type, area_m2 data: _operations-templates/<type>.yaml (one per intervention, 6 files) output: ops_y1_team, ops_y1_cost_brl, ops_y2_3_team, ops_y2_3_cost_brl, ops_y3plus_team + governance_model, ops_5yr_total_brl
User confirms operations · agent moves to sustainability

SustainabilityModelPicker · NEW

5 patterns based on what we found in the Brazilian community NBS literature: community-fund, municipal-cost-share, fee-based, grant-cycles, PES. Plus "outro" as escape hatch.

Multi-select with a single "primary" designation (the star). Most successful projects end up mixed — the literature confirms.

Monthly target uses band chips (not exact R$) to keep it low-friction and honest about uncertainty. Free-text revenue rationale captures the texture — feeds the E6 pitch.

component: SustainabilityModelPicker output: sustainability_models: ['community-fund', 'municipal-cost-share', 'grant-cycles'], sustainability_primary: 'community-fund', monthly_target_brl_band: '500-2000', revenue_rationale: "..."
