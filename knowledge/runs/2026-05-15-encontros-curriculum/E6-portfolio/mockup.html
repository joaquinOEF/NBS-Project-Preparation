ProjectCardPDF · NEW · 1-page funder-grade

Pulls from every phase. 5 visible sections + footer. Renders in the doc panel as a live preview; "Baixar PDF" calls server-side render.

Designed to map cleanly to the 2-min pitch — each section is one talking point. Funders who see lots of these will recognize the structure (similar to BPJP/C40 concept-note compressed onto 1 page).

Footer carries the maturity scorecard (17/27 · Building) — funders' shortcut for "what stage is this project at."

component: ProjectCardPDF mode: 'preview' | 'download' data: all phases of CBO profile + computed sections: header · site · intervention · impact · operations+ask · footer PDF: server-side via existing concept-note PDF infrastructure (reuse, don't rebuild)
