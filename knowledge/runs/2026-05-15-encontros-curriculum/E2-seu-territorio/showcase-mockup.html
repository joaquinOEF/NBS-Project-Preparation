Photo curation

Verified photo

3 of 4 cards use verified-source photos from the manifest at docs/photo-curation.md. Photographer + license badge in the bottom-right corner — non-intrusive but always visible.

The cards above use SVG approximations because the live photos aren't embedded in this static HTML. In the app they render the real /assets/interventions/*.jpg files.

Placeholder (Vila Flores)

Vila Flores Várzea Lab doesn't yet have a verified photo, so it ships with a gradient + emoji placeholder. The card is still useful (text describes the case) — it just doesn't claim to show a specific image.

When a photo is sourced and verified, the manifest entry switches from placeholder to photo and the card flips automatically.
